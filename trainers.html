COACHING STANDARD

What every session gets.

No coach runs a different club. These four things are non-negotiable across every program.

01

A clear plan

You know what you are doing, why it is on the board, and what next week looks like.

02

Real cues

One or two things to focus on at a time. Technique before load, always.

03

A recorded lift

Key sets are logged so progress is visible and decisions are based on data.
