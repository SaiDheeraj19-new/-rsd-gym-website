ABOUT FORGE

A room built
around the work.

No mirrors full of noise. No mystery programming. Just a clean training floor, coaches who pay attention, and a plan you can follow.

We coach the person, not the trend.

Forge started as a small training group in Kurnool. The idea was simple: make strength training understandable, progressive, and sustainable for ordinary schedules.

Today the club runs three tracks — strength, conditioning, and mobility — with the same standard across all of them. Every session has a purpose, every block has a progression, and every member gets a starting point that matches their body.

01 / CLEARSimple programming you can understand and repeat.
02 / COACHEDHands-on cues, not a screen and a stopwatch.
03 / PATIENTProgress that survives busy weeks and bad sleep.
04 / HONESTNo shortcuts, no fake timelines, no pressure.

THE SHORT VERSION

  • 2024 / THE IDEAA small group training in a shared space, testing simple strength blocks.
  • 2025 / THE COACHINGAdding mobility and conditioning so members could train more often without breaking down.
  • 2026 / THE CLUBForge opens with three tracks, four coaches, and a focus on repeatable progress.
