COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you join.

Still unsure? Send a message from the contact page and we will point you to the right starting track.

Q / 01

Do I need experience?

No. Foundation is designed for a first barbell session. We start with movement, not max lifts.

Q / 02

Can I change plans?

Yes. Move up or down at the end of any month. There is no lock-in and no downgrade fee.

Q / 03

Is there a joining fee?

No joining fee. The first walkthrough is free, and your plan starts when you decide to train.

Q / 04

How do I pay?

This demo does not process payments. In a real club, payment would be collected at the front desk or through a secure gateway.
